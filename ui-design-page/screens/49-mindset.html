Why it changes

Today's Journey tab mixes three things: a progress strip, the day's reading, and a list of chapters. That makes it read like a planner. This page keeps only the reading, and gives it the room a reading deserves: the line on the painting, the piece set like a book, and the ideas so far as framed cards you can come back to.

What's on the screen
  • Today's line on the painting, large, with who said it and where.
  • Today's piece opens like the first page of a book: the title, a gold drop cap and the first lines, then "Keep reading".
  • Ideas so far. Every day reached, as a framed card: the day in Roman numerals, the idea as its title, and the line. Filter by voice (Epictetus, Seneca, Marcus Aurelius). Tomorrow is a line, not a lock.
  • The reader. Full screen, serif, a drop cap, the quote as a pull-quote, a text size switch, and the previous and next day at the bottom, so reading on is one tap.
Taken out
  • The chapter list, the chapter card, the day counter and the progress bars. Chapters and rank stay where they matter: the quest and Me.
  • No ticks, no "read" marks and no counts of what was read. "5 ideas" is the days reached, not a score.
In the iOS app
  • Same data as now: the Journey response already carries today's piece and every day's line; earlier bodies load when opened. No backend change.
  • The tab is renamed Mindset (book icon kept). VoiceOver reads each card as "Day 4, The Trade Was Never the Beginning, Epictetus".
