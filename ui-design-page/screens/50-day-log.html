Why it changes

A grid of boxes is a record, not a reason to come back. Here the month is a fresco behind frosted glass, and each day you judge clears one pane, whatever the grade. A fully judged month shows the whole painting. The page sits on glass over the painting, like the rest of the app's art pages.

The colours: from sun to sea
  • A · Clean: Sun gold #F5C451. Every rule kept, or no trade.
  • B · Solid: Champagne #F6E1A6. Kept, with a wobble.
  • C · Mixed: Moon silver #D3DDE6. One rule slipped.
  • D · Slipped: Lilac #B9A5F0. Two or more slipped.
  • E · Tilt: Aegean #74B3E8. The calm blue of the calm screen.
  • Warm light for kept days, cooling toward the sea as rules slip. No red, no green: this is not a P&L. Each colour is light, so it reads on dark glass.
  • The grade is always its letter, set in its own light: a lettered chip on the fresco, a four-point star in the week. Colour is never the only signal.
  • An unjudged day stays frosted, never coloured as bad.
