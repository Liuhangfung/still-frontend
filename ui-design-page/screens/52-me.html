    Why it changes

    Me mixed two jobs: what the practice has made of you, and the switches for the app. The switches won, and the rank was a crest above a settings list. Now Me is only about you, and every setting is one tap away behind the gear.

    On Me
    • The crest under the ceiling painting, with the day, the chapter and the XP.
    • The road to S: every rank, where you are, and the next boss that earns the next one. Rank never comes from XP.
    • Your five as a pentagon: the shape of what you've practised, read against the largest of the five. Tap a stat for what grows it.
    • Your practice: four plain counts from Journey, Log and Notes. No streaks.
    • The trader you said you were: the questionnaire read back, so later reviews have something to compare with.
    • A quieter pace stays one tap from Me (design 45 says it must always be easy to reach); it opens Settings at those two switches.
    In Settings
    • A quieter pace (calm coach, pause), coach, appearance, export my notes, time zone, sign out, version.
    • A pushed page with a back button, not a sheet, so a switch can't be swiped away half-changed.
    • Same data as now: GET /v1/me/stats, the profile, Journey, Day Log and inventory. Changing the coach needs a profile update the API may not have yet.

    Design preview only. Nothing is saved or sent. Artwork is concept-only pending production rights clearance.
