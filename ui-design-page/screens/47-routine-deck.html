How the deck works

Same Today page as screen 08. The three Morning / Market review / Evening lists are replaced by one small stack of cards, one painting each, in the order of the day. The full list (screen 37) stays one tap away for anyone who prefers it.

Gestures
  • Swipe right · Start. The card leaves the deck and drops into an "In progress" row underneath. Tap the row to open its check-in (the same taps as screens 38–42). Saving marks it done and removes the row; closing without saving keeps it in progress.
  • Swipe left · Later. The card goes to the back of the deck. It is not skipped: the programme says the seven are done every day, so "later" never removes one, never costs XP and never says "skipped". It comes round again.
  • Tap · Details. What to do, why it matters, time and XP, with Start and Later buttons. Reading is never counted as doing.
  • Swipe only, no buttons. While dragging, the card washes green from the right or red from the left, stronger the further it goes. It only commits past a third of the card's width; let go earlier and it springs back.
Calm rules kept
  • Red and green appear only during the drag, as a direction signal. Nothing red stays on screen afterwards, and the words stay "Later" and "Start", never "skip" or "reject".
  • Saved check-ins leave the "In progress" rows and fill the gold segments at the top. Once every card is picked, the deck says so; the last save turns it into the "whole set" card (+5 DIS once).
  • Lite shows only Mind Check, News Scan, Fit-to-Trade Gate and Rule Check.
In the iOS app
  • No buttons on screen, but VoiceOver gets "Start", "Later" and "Details" actions on the card, so nobody has to drag. Colour is never the only signal: the card also flies in that direction. Reduce Motion fades cards instead of flinging them.
  • Haptics: a light tick when a drag crosses the line, a firm tap when a check-in saves, nothing for Later.
  • Each sub-task needs its own painting (artwork rights are still pending); this preview reuses existing crops.
  • No backend change: "In progress" and Later are kept on this phone (until the 04:00 reset), and the row opens the existing check-in. Saved state still comes from GET /v1/subtasks/today.
