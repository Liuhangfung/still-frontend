    Why it changes

    Screen 09 reads like a worksheet: a heading, a radio, a numbered list, a "You'll make" list. Same content here, shaped like a quest: a place (the painting), a voice (the coach), a road (the steps) and something to carry home (the tools).

    What's on the screen
    • Painted hero. The quest's painting full-bleed, with the title on the art, the goal in one line and gold dashes for progress. The back chip floats on the art.
    • The coach's line as an epigraph card lifted onto the painting, not a grey aside.
    • Your path. Steps on a gold trail. Done steps get a gold tick and a solid line; the step you're on opens into a card with its own crop, the instructions in two lines and one Begin button; later steps stay quiet. How each is answered shows as an icon: read, tap, write, or notebook.
    • Short fades the steps it leaves out ("Not in Short") instead of hiding them, so the path keeps its shape.
    • You'll take away shows the two tools as framed, greyed paintings with a lock. Finishing colours them in and swaps the lock for a tick.
    • The dock names the next step ("Up next · Find your urge story"), and turns gold as "Finish quest" once every step is done.
    Rules kept
    • Opening a step never completes it; reading never counts as doing. Steps can be done in any order: every row opens, only the highlight follows the first open one.
    • XP is described as "up to" and participation only; the finish receipt shows what the server actually paid.
    • Notebook steps still offer the app: writing stays on the phone, end-to-end encrypted if synced.
    In the iOS app
    • Reuses ArtCrop, the art scrim colours and the painted resume bar from screen 47. The objective screen itself (fields) is unchanged in this pass.
    • The trail and nodes are decoration: VoiceOver reads each step as "Step 3 of 5, Find your urge story, 3 minutes, in app, not done". Status is never colour alone (tick, number, words).
    • No backend change: everything here is already in the quest payload.

    Design preview only. Nothing is saved or sent and no XP is awarded. Artwork is concept-only pending production rights clearance.
