Art sets the atmosphere. Practice gives it meaning.

These pages normalize the first-pass image concepts into a shared, editable interface. They are independent examples, not a connected native app. Radio choices, checkboxes, steppers, optional fields and rule ordering work locally; primary save/start/account actions show a preview notice instead of submitting data.

The reviewed backend currently supplies Chapter 1 / Days 1–14. Market-neutral language covers stocks, forex, crypto, futures, options and commodities, but some source enums and authored content remain crypto-first. No live market data, broker connector, automatic trade verification or in-app AI is implied.

Files, fonts and artwork rights

Open index.html directly, keeping the whole ui-design-page folder together. Individual pages are in screens/; shared styles and JavaScript are in shared/; local fonts and artwork are in assets/. Display type is Cormorant Garamond; controls use Manrope, both under the included SIL Open Font License notices. Artwork and image references are concept-only pending production-rights clearance.
