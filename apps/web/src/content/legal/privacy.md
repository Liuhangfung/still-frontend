## The short version

- We collect **as little as we can**. Still is about how you trade, not how much money you have.
- **We never ask for your balances, positions, profits, losses, debts or exchange logins**, and we never connect to a broker or exchange.
- **Anything you type in a quest stays on your phone.** Our servers only store your taps, choices and the tools you build (like your Rules Card).
- **We don't sell your data**, and we don't use it for advertising.
- You can **delete your account and your data inside the app** at any time.

## 1. Who this policy covers

It covers this website and the Still app for iPhone. You must be **18 or older** to use Still.

## 2. What we collect

### On this website

| Data | Why | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| Your **email address**, if you join the founder list | To send you the Day 1 quest, launch news and your founder price | Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it |
| What you send through the **contact form** (name if you give it, email, topic, message) | To reply to you | As long as needed to handle your request |
| Basic **technical logs** from our hosting provider (IP address, browser type, pages requested) | To keep the site running and secure | A short period set by the hosting provider |

The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

### In the app

| Data | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Account** | Email address, or your Apple ID sign-in (Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one) | To create your account and let you log in |
| **Profile and settings** | Time zone, chosen coach, reminder time, language, your 56-day path | To build your programme and know when "today" is for you |
| **Questionnaire answers** | Your trading habits in ranges and taps (for example "how many trades last week: 6–15"), never money amounts. "Don't know" and "Prefer not to say" are always options | To personalise your quests |
| **Progress** | Quests done, choices and scales you tapped, XP, rank, day grades (A–E) and tags | To run the programme and show your progress |
| **Tools you build** | Trading Plan, Pre-Trade Checklist, Rules Card, Trigger Map | So you keep them and can export them |
| **Purchases** | Which product you bought and whether it's active | To unlock what you paid for. **Payments are handled by Apple**; we never see your card details |
| **Notifications** | A push notification token for your device | To send the reminders you asked for |
| **App analytics** | Events like "quest completed" or "screen opened", without the content of what you entered | To find out what works and fix what doesn't |
| **Crash reports** | Technical details of an error, with personal data removed | To fix bugs |

### What we do **not** collect

- Anything you **type in a quest** (reasons, reflections, letters) and **Day Log notes**. These are stored **only on your device**. If you lose or reset your phone, they are lost unless you exported them.
- Money amounts, balances, positions, profits, losses, income, debts or where your money comes from.
- Exchange or broker logins, API keys or trade history from your accounts.
- Your contacts, photos, location or microphone.

### If you tell the app you're struggling

Some screens let you say things like "I just had a big loss" or "I can't stop". We use that only to switch the coach to **calm mode** and show you where to find help. It is never used for marketing, and it is never shared.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:

- **export** your tools (the app has an export) and ask for the rest of your data,

- **complain** to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email **{{privacyEmail}}**. We'll reply within the time the law requires.

## 8. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, access controls, separate test and live systems, and by keeping secret keys out of the app. Login tokens are stored in your iPhone's secure Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep risks low and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.
