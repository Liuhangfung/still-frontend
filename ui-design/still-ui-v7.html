Both use icons drawn for Still (the Log icon is four manga panels, Path is a route to a flag), filled in when active. They are calm and solid, not JoJo: the bar is furniture, the screens are the show.

Option D, in short

Logo as the Log tab

The centre slot is the Still pause logo. Logging your day is the brand promise: pause, look at how you traded.

Icons only

No labels: five shapes people learn in a day. The screen title always says where you are.

Floating capsule

Dark, slightly see-through, fully rounded. It sits above the content instead of cutting the screen off.

One thing to check

The floating Urge button also uses the pause bars. With the logo in the bar, the Urge button should keep its "URGE?" label so the two are never confused.
