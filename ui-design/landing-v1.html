FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. Still is habit training. It never gives signals, price targets or tips, and never tells you what to buy or sell.

Do I have to trade to use it?

No. No quest ever requires a real-money trade. "Not trading today" is always a valid answer and earns the same XP. Week 1 is a no-trade week.

Which markets is it for?

Crypto first, and the habits apply to any market. US stocks content is coming next.

What if I miss a day?

You get an easy Recovery Quest. Progress is never wiped and you are never shamed.

How much time does it take?

About 10 minutes for the daily routine and 5–15 minutes for the main quest. Every quest has a 5-minute short version for busy days.

What happens after day 56?

You keep your rank, tools and history. If you want to keep going, an optional yearly membership is offered at day 56.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Full refund within 7 days of purchase.
