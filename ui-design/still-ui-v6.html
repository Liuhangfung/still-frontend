STILL: UI design v6 · Day Log, JoJo style

The v5 Day Log redrawn with JoJo's own visual language. The 5 colours become grades A to E, like a JoJo stat card. Days are drawn as manga panels, the verdict hits like an impact frame, and the day closes with the "To Be Continued" arrow. Same rules as v5: no money, same XP for any grade, and calm mode after a tilt day.

A–E GRADES

Stat-card letters. A gold · B teal · C grey · D violet · E coral.

MANGA PANELS

Skewed frames, diagonal gutters, screentone dots and hatching.

IMPACT FRAMES

Radial speed lines and one giant letter for the big moment.

ゴゴゴ · ドン!

Katakana sound effects in a heavy Japanese display font.

「NAMEPLATES」

Narrator captions and bracketed name tags, like the anime.

TO BE CONTINUED ⟵

Closes every logged day. Tomorrow is the next episode.
